# 180-Day Implementation Roadmap

## Days 0–30: Mobilize
- Appoint sponsor and program owner.
- Approve charter, scope, taxonomy, risk appetite and RACI.
- Identify authoritative vendor and contract data sources.
- Establish minimum onboarding gate and emergency exception process.

## Days 31–60: Inventory and segment
- Consolidate vendors, owners, spend, services, integrations and data flows.
- Perform initial tiering and identify critical fourth-party concentration.
- Prioritize unassessed critical and high-risk vendors.

## Days 61–90: Operationalize
- Deploy intake, due diligence, finding and acceptance workflows.
- Approve contract standards and escalation SLAs.
- Train analysts, procurement and business owners.
- Launch endpoint-security deep-dive for privileged agents.

## Days 91–120: Monitor
- Enroll critical vendors in external, financial, legal and service monitoring.
- Establish incident coordination and material-change triggers.
- Build dashboards and monthly steering committee reporting.

## Days 121–180: Assure and improve
- Test critical-vendor continuity and exit plans.
- Perform quality assurance sampling.
- Conduct tabletop exercises.
- Complete internal audit readiness review and maturity baseline.
- Set annual improvement backlog.
