# Authoritative References

Use the latest official edition and verify applicability before implementation.

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0 — governance, supply-chain outcomes and enterprise cybersecurity risk taxonomy.
- NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1 Update 1 — cybersecurity supply-chain risk management strategy, plans, assessments and controls.
- NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 — Supply Chain Risk Management (SR) family and related acquisition, contingency, incident, access and system-integrity controls.
- NIST SP 800-218 SSDF Version 1.1 — secure software development and supplier/acquirer communication. Verify the publication status of later SSDF versions with NIST before citing them as final.
- CISA ICT Supply Chain Risk Management resources, including vendor assessment templates.
- ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO/IEC 27036 series — information security and supplier relationship concepts.
- AICPA SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria — independent assurance where scope is applicable.

## Use limitations

Framework mapping is informative, not a certification statement. The repository does not reproduce proprietary standards. Legal, regulatory, contractual and sector-specific requirements must be added by each adopting organization.
