# Metrics and Reporting

## Board-level KRIs
- Percentage of critical services dependent on a single vendor or common cloud/subprocessor.
- Critical vendors above appetite or operating under expired acceptance.
- Material third-party incidents and business impact.
- Critical services without tested exit or continuity plans.
- Aggregate exposure by geography, technology, data class, and fourth party.

## Program KPIs
- Inventory completeness and ownership coverage.
- Intake completed before contract signature.
- Assessment cycle time by tier.
- Percentage of assessments supported by current independent evidence.
- Findings overdue, aging, recurrence and closure validation rate.
- Contract security clause and incident-notification compliance.
- Reassessments completed on time.
- Monitoring alerts investigated within SLA.
- Access and data deletion validated at termination.

## Quality metrics
- Tiering overrides and rationale.
- Reviewer disagreement rate.
- Reopened findings.
- False-positive/false-negative monitoring outcomes.
- Assessment sampling defects.

## Reporting rule
Metrics must show trend, target, appetite threshold, data quality, accountable owner, corrective action and forecast—not only a point-in-time count.
