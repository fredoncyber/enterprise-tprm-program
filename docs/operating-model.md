# Operating Model

## Three lines

| Line | Accountability |
|---|---|
| First line | Business owner, procurement, technology owner, data owner, vendor manager implement controls and own risk. |
| Second line | TPRM, security risk, privacy, compliance, resilience and legal establish methodology, challenge evidence and monitor exposure. |
| Third line | Internal audit independently evaluates program design and operating effectiveness. |

## Core roles
- **Board/Risk Committee:** approves appetite and oversees material exposure.
- **Executive sponsor:** resolves resource and cross-functional barriers.
- **TPRM program owner:** methodology, inventory, workflow, quality assurance, reporting.
- **TPRM analyst:** scoping, evidence review, risk analysis, findings and monitoring.
- **Business relationship owner:** business justification, performance, remediation and exit ownership.
- **Procurement:** sourcing gates, contract process, approved channels.
- **Legal/Privacy:** clauses, data-processing terms, regulatory and transfer analysis.
- **Information Security:** technical requirements and validation.
- **Business Continuity:** resilience, dependency mapping and testing.
- **Finance:** viability, insurance, fraud and concentration review.

## Segregation of duties
An analyst should not approve a risk acceptance they prepared. Critical-risk decisions require independent second-line approval and executive acceptance. Procurement cannot bypass required risk gates solely because of commercial deadlines.
