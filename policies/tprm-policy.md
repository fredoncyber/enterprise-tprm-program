# Third-Party Risk Management Policy

**Owner:** Enterprise Risk / Information Security  
**Approver:** Board or delegated Risk Committee  
**Review:** At least annually and upon material regulatory or business change

## Policy statements
1. All third parties must be recorded in the authoritative inventory with an accountable business owner.
2. Risk intake and inherent-risk tiering must occur before selection, contract, production access, data exchange, or material expansion.
3. Due diligence must be proportional to inherent risk and cover applicable cyber, privacy, resilience, legal, compliance, operational, financial, geopolitical, AI, concentration, and fourth-party domains.
4. Critical and high-risk vendors require evidence-based assessment and documented residual-risk approval.
5. Contracts must include approved risk clauses proportionate to service exposure.
6. Access must be least privilege, time-bound where practical, monitored, and removed at termination.
7. Third parties must notify the organization of security/privacy incidents and material changes within contractually defined periods.
8. Findings require owners, severity, target dates, treatment plans, evidence, and validated closure.
9. Risk acceptance must identify the specific exposure, compensating controls, accountable executive, expiry and review trigger.
10. Critical third parties must have continuity, recovery, concentration and exit plans that are periodically tested.
11. Monitoring frequency must align with vendor tier and emerging risk.
12. Emergency onboarding requires documented executive exception and retrospective assessment within a defined period.
13. Policy violations may result in access suspension, purchasing restriction, escalation, non-renewal or termination.

## Exceptions
Exceptions are time-limited and cannot waive statutory obligations, sanctions restrictions, or mandatory prohibitions.
