# Third-Party Risk Acceptance

- Vendor, service and business owner
- Specific risk statement and affected assets/objectives
- Inherent and residual rating
- Evidence and unresolved findings
- Reason remediation is not currently feasible
- Alternatives considered
- Compensating controls and monitoring
- Maximum exposure period and expiry date
- Material-change and incident revocation triggers
- Legal/privacy/security concurrence
- Accountable executive acceptance
