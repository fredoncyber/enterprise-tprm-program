# Vendor Intake Questionnaire

## Business context
- Vendor legal name, service, business owner, procurement owner and target go-live.
- Business objective and consequence if unavailable.
- Alternatives considered and exit strategy.

## Data
- Data types, classification, subjects, volume, residency, retention and deletion.
- Whether the vendor is controller, processor, service provider or independent recipient.

## Access and architecture
- Networks, applications, APIs, endpoints, identities, privileges, agents and administrative access.
- Deployment diagram and trust boundaries.

## Operations
- Critical processes supported, RTO/RPO, transaction volumes, peak periods and manual workaround.

## Compliance
- Applicable laws, contracts, industry standards, export/sanctions, records and audit requirements.

## Supply chain
- Hosting providers, critical subprocessors, software components, resellers and support locations.

## AI
- AI/model use, training data, prompts, automated decisions, model providers, retention, human oversight and opt-out controls.
