# Contract Security Schedule Checklist

- Defined services, data, systems, locations and subprocessors
- Minimum security controls and secure-development obligations
- Identity, access, encryption, logging and vulnerability requirements
- Security/privacy incident notification, updates and cooperation
- Evidence, audit, testing and regulatory access rights
- Subprocessor approval/notice and flow-down obligations
- Data use, AI training restrictions, residency, retention, return and deletion
- Availability, RTO/RPO, backup, DR tests and service credits
- Change notification, end-of-life and vulnerability disclosure
- Cyber insurance and indemnity as approved by Legal
- Termination assistance, portability, agent removal and continued protection
- Order of precedence, survival and remedy provisions
