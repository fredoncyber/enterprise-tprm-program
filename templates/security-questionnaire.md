7. Vulnerability, patch and penetration-test management

9. Data classification, minimization, retention, deletion and DLP
10. Backup, recovery, resilience, capacity and disaster recovery tests
11. Personnel screening, training and insider-risk controls
12. Physical and environmental controls
13. Subprocessor/fourth-party governance
14. Regulatory compliance and customer audit support
15. Product end-of-life, portability, termination and secure deletion
16. AI governance, model security and data-use restrictions
