# Third-Party Incident Response Procedure

1. Activate security incident process and third-party contacts.
2. Confirm affected service, tenants, data, identities, endpoints and time window.
3. Establish secure communications and evidence preservation.
4. Require facts: indicators, root cause status, containment, scope, data impact, persistence, update integrity and fourth-party involvement.
5. Apply containment: disable integration, rotate credentials, isolate endpoints, restrict data flow, fail over or suspend service.
6. Coordinate legal, privacy, regulatory, insurance, communications and law enforcement obligations.
7. Track vendor actions and independent validation.
8. Reassess risk, contract compliance and relationship viability.
9. Complete post-incident review, corrective actions and control updates.
