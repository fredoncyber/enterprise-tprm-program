# Continuous Monitoring Procedure

## Signals
Security advisories, breaches, exploited vulnerabilities, attack-surface changes, leaked credentials, domain/certificate changes, financial distress, sanctions, litigation, regulatory action, ownership changes, outages, SLA degradation, subprocessor changes, assurance report expiry, insurance lapse and material product changes.

## Workflow
1. Ingest and deduplicate signal.
2. Verify source and affected service/version/tenant.
3. Determine exposure and materiality.
4. Contact vendor using severity-based SLA.
5. Apply containment or compensating controls.
6. Open finding/incident/material-change reassessment.
7. Update residual risk and decision.
8. Document disposition and lessons learned.

External ratings are indicators, not conclusive evidence. Vendors must have an opportunity to clarify inaccurate or non-applicable observations.
