# Assessment Procedure

1. Validate intake completeness and business owner.
2. Assign tier using inherent-risk criteria and overrides.
3. Create domain-specific evidence plan.
4. Request documents through approved secure channel.
5. Review assurance scope, dates, exclusions, subservice treatment and exceptions.
6. Compare questionnaire responses with evidence, architecture and known events.
7. Conduct interviews and technical validation where warranted.
8. Record control design, implementation, operating effectiveness and evidence confidence.
9. Write atomic risk findings with condition, criteria, cause, consequence and recommendation.
10. Calculate and challenge residual risk.
11. Obtain remediation commitment and contractual protection.
12. Route decision according to approval matrix.
13. Store evidence and decision record with retention controls.
14. Enroll vendor in monitoring and reassessment schedule.
15. Perform independent quality review for Tier 1 and selected Tier 2 assessments.
