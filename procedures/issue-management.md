# Third-Party Issue Management

## Severity
- **Critical:** active exploitation, material control absence, imminent severe impact, or exposure outside non-delegable appetite.
- **High:** significant likelihood/impact requiring executive visibility and near-term treatment.
- **Moderate:** meaningful weakness with bounded exposure.
- **Low:** limited weakness or improvement opportunity.

## Required record
Finding ID, vendor, service, risk statement, evidence, affected requirement, severity, owner, action, compensating control, due date, status, verification and closure approver.

## Governance
- Critical findings: immediate escalation; onboarding blocked unless emergency authority approves.
- High findings: written treatment and senior owner approval.
- Extensions require changed facts, updated residual risk and new approval.
- Closure requires evidence validation; vendor assertion alone is insufficient.
- Repeated extensions or recurring findings trigger tier and relationship review.
