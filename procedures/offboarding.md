# Offboarding and Exit Procedure

- Confirm termination authority, dates and continuity plan.
- Inventory vendor identities, API keys, agents, certificates, tunnels, applications, devices, data stores and subprocessors.
- Export required business records in usable format.
- Revoke logical/physical access and disable integrations.
- Remove endpoint agents safely and validate replacement coverage.
- Recover assets and settle licenses/accounts.
- Obtain and validate data return/deletion certification, including backups and fourth parties where applicable.
- Preserve required legal/audit records.
- Monitor for residual callbacks, credentials and access attempts.
- Document completion, exceptions and lessons learned.
